See your Google Photos on your Strava activities

Your photos live in Google Photos and your routes live in Strava, and nothing connects the two. Takeout Photo's Activities app links them on your own computer: it reads your Google Photos from a Takeout export, syncs your activities from Strava (official API, OAuth), Komoot or Garmin Connect (your own login), and matches each photo to an activity by capture time, placing it along the GPS track.

Why photos and routes end up in two places

You take photos with your phone, so they end up in Google Photos. You record your runs, rides and hikes with Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect, so the routes end up there. Your activity app shows the photos you uploaded to it (Strava shows the ones you uploaded to Strava), but the rest of your camera roll (the photos from your Google Photos library) stays invisible on the activity. Takeout Photo closes that gap locally, without uploading anything anywhere.

How the matching works

The Activities app connects to your account: with Strava it uses the official Strava API and OAuth — you create your own free Strava API application (the app includes a step-by-step guide) and authorize Takeout Photo; with Komoot or Garmin Connect you sign in with your own login (Komoot login; Garmin Connect email and password, plus an MFA code if you have one enabled) over their unofficial APIs. The app then syncs your activities from every connected provider; activities that appear in more than one service are de-duplicated. For each activity, your Google Photos from the Takeout are matched by capture time: every photo taken during the activity is placed at the point along the GPS track where it was taken, shown as "km 12.3 · 10:42".

Because the capture time comes from the Takeout JSON sidecars (not the file timestamp), the match is based on when the photo was really taken.

Komoot and Garmin Connect work the same way

The Activities app is not Strava-only. Sign in with your Komoot login, or with your Garmin Connect email and password (plus an MFA code if you have multi-factor authentication enabled), and your Komoot or Garmin Connect activities sync exactly like Strava ones: matched to your Takeout photos by capture time and placed along the GPS track (GPS photos by distance to the track, photos without GPS interpolated between their neighbours), shown together with your Strava activities on one map of all your routes, in one activities list, with stats and a heatmap, and de-duplicated when the same activity appears in more than one service. Komoot and Garmin Connect use unofficial APIs; Takeout Photo is not affiliated with or endorsed by Komoot or Garmin.

What you get

  • A map of all your routes, coloured by sport, with a heatmap mode.
  • Filters by sport, year, country, and "with photos".
  • Activity pages with the route, the best photos for that activity, and a photo strip.
  • Stats per year, and start/end place names for each activity.
  • Photos you already uploaded to Strava are shown too; duplicates of your Takeout photos are hidden so nothing appears twice.
  • A link back to the activity on its provider (Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect), plus an optional embed where the provider offers one.
  • Activities are linked to the trips in the Photos app, so a weekend ride and the holiday around it connect.

How to connect your activity accounts

  1. Export your Google Photos with Google Takeout (see how to export and browse a Takeout).
  2. Download the app (Mac: Apple silicon, macOS 12 or later; Windows: 10 or 11, 64-bit) and point it at the archives; free to explore the sample library. Lite (free) imports your own Takeout for the photo timeline; activity matching needs a plan (pricing).
  3. Connect a provider in the Activities app:
    • Strava: create your own free Strava API application (a step-by-step guide is built into the app) and authorize Takeout Photo to read your activities via OAuth.
    • Komoot: sign in with your Komoot login.
    • Garmin Connect: sign in with your Garmin email and password, and enter the MFA code if you have multi-factor authentication enabled.
  4. Start the sync.

Privacy

Everything is processed locally on your computer; your photos are never uploaded to our servers. For Strava the app reads activities and GPS tracks over the official API, read-only, and does not modify your Strava account. For Komoot and Garmin Connect the app talks directly from your computer to Komoot / Garmin with your own login (unofficial APIs); your credentials and tokens are sent only to Komoot / Garmin and stored only on your device — we never receive them. See the privacy policy.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create my own Strava API application?

Yes, for Strava. Strava requires every app to use its own free API application; the Takeout Photo app includes a step-by-step guide for creating one and authorizing it via OAuth. Komoot and Garmin Connect do not need this: you just sign in with your own login.

Does it work with Komoot or Garmin Connect too?

Yes. The Activities app also syncs your Komoot activities (sign in with your Komoot login) and your Garmin Connect activities (email and password, plus an MFA code if you have multi-factor authentication enabled), over their unofficial APIs. Matching works the same way, and activities from all providers are shown together and de-duplicated.

How are my photos matched to an activity?

By capture time. Each Google Photo taken while the activity was recorded — on Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect — is placed along the activity's GPS track at the point matching its time (for example "km 12.3 · 10:42").

Does it upload my photos to Strava, Komoot or Garmin Connect?

No. The app works locally and never uploads your photos to any of them: for Strava it shows the photos you already uploaded to Strava as well, and hides duplicates of the same photos from your Takeout so nothing appears twice.

Which activities can have photos?

Any activity with a GPS track and a time range. The map colours routes by sport and you can filter by sport, year, country, or only activities with photos.

Is my activity data kept private?

Yes. Strava activities are read over the official Strava API with read-only access; Komoot and Garmin Connect are contacted directly from your computer with your own login (unofficial APIs), and their credentials and tokens stay on your device. Everything is processed on your own computer, and your photos are never uploaded to our servers or to the activity providers by the app.
