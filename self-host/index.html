Run Takeout Photo on a NAS with Docker Compose

Beta: the Docker image is in beta and will be published at ghcr.io/ecyshor/takepho (amd64; arm64 later). It gives you the same app, and the same three apps, running on your NAS or home server, open in a browser on your network. Your scheduled Takeout exports can land in a folder on the NAS.

What you get

The same app as the desktop version: the Photos timeline, Strava (photos on activities) and Proton Photos sync, served from your NAS and opened in a browser at http://<nas-address>:8080. Everything is processed on the NAS; your photos are never uploaded to our servers.

Plans and account

The same plans and prices as the desktop app. Sign in from Settings → Account in the browser and choose a plan. The NAS counts as one of your account's 2 computers. It needs internet at least once a month to refresh the licence.

Requirements

  • Docker Engine with Compose v2.24 or later (Synology Container Manager, Unraid, TrueNAS, or any Linux box).
  • x86-64 (amd64) CPU.
  • 4 GB RAM free for the container; 8 GB recommended for big libraries.
  • Disk for the data folder (thumbnails and indexes, plus about 600 MB for the AI model on first use).
  • Optional: an NVIDIA GPU (driver 580 or later, NVIDIA Container Toolkit) with the image ghcr.io/ecyshor/takepho-cuda:latest.

Quick start

Save this as docker-compose.yml on the NAS:

services:
  app:
    image: ghcr.io/ecyshor/takepho:latest
    restart: unless-stopped
    ports:
      - "${TIMELINE_PORT:-8080}:8000"
    volumes:
      - ${TIMELINE_DATA:-timeline-data}:/data
      - ${TIMELINE_MODELS:-timeline-models}:/app/models/clip
      - ${TAKEOUT_DIR:-./takeout}:/takeout:ro
volumes:
  timeline-data: {}
  timeline-models: {}

Then start it and open the app:

docker compose up -d
# open http://<nas-address>:8080 in your browser

Folders

  • /data: the library database, thumbnails and indexes. Back this up.
  • /app/models/clip: the downloaded AI model. It can be re-downloaded, no backup needed.
  • /takeout: your Takeout archives, mounted read-only. On Synology, point TAKEOUT_DIR at for example /volume1/takeout.

Security

The app has no login of its own. Keep it on your home network, or reach it through a VPN (Tailscale, WireGuard) or a reverse proxy with authentication. Never forward the port to the internet.

Updating and backups

Update with:

docker compose pull && docker compose up -d

For backups, copy the data volume (timeline-data).

Synology, Unraid, TrueNAS

In Synology Container Manager, Unraid or TrueNAS, create a Compose project (or stack) from the file above and start it.

Help

If something does not work, open a support issue with where the app runs, the version, what happened and the steps to reproduce.
