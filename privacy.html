Takeout Photo

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

Summary

Takeout Photo runs on your computer and processes your data there. We (the developer) do not receive, store, or sell your photos, metadata, location data or Google account data. There are no analytics, ads or tracking in the app. If you buy a plan, the account service at account.takepho.com stores only your sign-in identity, your purchase records, and your registered Macs (see "Account and purchases" below). This website counts visits with cookieless analytics (see "This website" below) and shows no ads.

Data the app processes locally

The app processes your Google Takeout archives (photos, videos, and their metadata such as dates and GPS coordinates), optional Google Maps Timeline / location-history files you add, and the results it derives (trips, places, thumbnails, AI curation scores). This data is stored only in the app's data folder on your computer; you can delete it any time by deleting that folder or using the app's reset function.

Google user data

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests the drive.file scope, which lets it see only the Google Drive files you explicitly pick in Google's file picker (your Takeout archives). It uses this access only to download those archives to your computer for processing. It does not access other files, does not modify, upload or delete anything in your Drive, and does not access Gmail, Contacts, or Google Photos.

An advanced mode lets technical users plug in their own Google Cloud OAuth client with read-only Drive access (drive.readonly); in that case the access goes to their own client and is used the same way.

OAuth tokens are stored only on your computer and are sent only to Google. You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions or by signing out in the app.

Takeout Photo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not read by humans.

Account and purchases (account.takepho.com)

To unlock a paid plan, you sign in to the account service at account.takepho.com with Google or Apple (from the app: Settings → Account). The app then works offline on a signed entitlement and refreshes it about once a week. Your photos never leave your computer — the account service never receives them.

The account service stores only:

  • Your sign-in identity — the provider (Google or Apple), the account id, and the email address as given by Google or Apple (Apple may give us a private relay address instead of your real one).
  • Your plan and purchase records from Stripe (which plans you bought, subscription status, refunds) — no card data: the card is held by Stripe/Link, not by us.
  • Your registered Macs — a random device id, the Mac's name, and the last-seen time. A plan covers 2 Macs per account.

Purposes and legal bases: we process this data to create and maintain your account, deliver and enforce your plan (2 Macs, entitlement refresh), and process payments — the performance of a contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). We keep security logs (failed sign-ins, rate limiting) based on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Hosting and backups: the account service is hosted on Hetzner in Germany. The database is backed up nightly to a Hetzner Storage Box (also in Germany, SSH-only access); backups are kept for 14 days.

Retention: account data is kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as required by tax and accounting law. Backups are deleted after 14 days.

Processors:

  • Hetzner (hosting, Germany) — see the Hetzner privacy policy.
  • Google and Apple — sign-in only; see the Google privacy policy and the Apple privacy policy.
  • Stripe / Link — payments as merchant of record (they hold the card data and charge you); see the Stripe privacy policy (which covers Link) and "Sold through Link".

Your rights: you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in Switzerland, the FDPIC). To delete your account and all its data, email us at hello@takepho.com from your account email — we will delete the account, its devices and purchase-linked personal data (keeping only what tax law requires us to keep).

Third-party services the app contacts

The app contacts the following third-party services:

  • Google: sign-in, and downloading the archives you picked or shared a Drive link to.
  • account.takepho.com (our account service, hosted on Hetzner in Germany): sign-in with Google or Apple, buying a plan, and refreshing the signed entitlement about once a week. No photos are sent.
  • Stripe / Link checkout: buying a plan opens Stripe's Managed Payments checkout in your browser (Link is the merchant of record); the card details go to Stripe/Link only.
  • takepho.com/appcast.xml and GitHub release downloads: the app's update feed (Sparkle) and the update files themselves, hosted on this website and GitHub.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim: to name places, the app sends approximate coordinates — rounded to about 1 km — of places in your photos, without photos or other identifiers. See the OSMF privacy policy.
  • OpenFreeMap: map tiles for the map view; your device requests tiles for the area you view. https://openfreemap.org
  • Hugging Face: one-time download of the open-source AI model used for curation; no user data sent.

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We set no cookies and use no advertising or cross-site trackers.

To count visits we use analiza.dev, a simple analytics service run by the developer. For each page view it records the page address, the referring page, your browser, operating system and device type (from the user agent) and your country (looked up from your IP address). To count unique visitors without cookies it stores a one-way hash of your IP address, user agent and browser language together with the current month, so the same visitor gets a new hash every month; your IP address itself is not stored. The data is used only for aggregate visit statistics and is not shared or sold.

GitHub may log IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

This app and website are not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when we make changes to our privacy practices.
